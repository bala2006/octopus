# Code of Conduct

Octopus welcomes contributors and users from different backgrounds and experience levels. All participation in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces should be respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, clear, and patient.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Give specific, actionable technical feedback rather than personal criticism.
- Respect different levels of experience and acknowledge others' work.
- Prioritize community safety and collaboration.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, discrimination, sexualized or unwelcome attention, bullying, repeated personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publication of private information without permission are not tolerated. Disagreement on implementation or design is welcome; harassment is not.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness a conduct issue, contact the repository maintainer **privately**, using a contact method listed on [@bala2006's GitHub profile](https://github.com/bala2006) when available. If no private contact method is available, use GitHub's [abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies), especially for immediate platform safety concerns. Avoid publishing sensitive personal information or misconduct reports in public issues.

Include relevant context, dates, links, and screenshots only when safe and appropriate to share. Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible, but confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when platform moderation or safety requires disclosure.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban repeated or severe violations. Decisions aim to be proportionate to the behavior and the safety of the community.

This policy applies in project-managed online and offline spaces and when representing Octopus publicly.
